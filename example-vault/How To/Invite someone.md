# Invite someone

Notes are shared the GitHub way: whoever can open the repo on GitHub can open it in the app.

## Steps

1. **Give them access on GitHub**: add them as a collaborator (the repo's **Settings › Collaborators**), or as a member of the organization that owns it.
2. **Send them an invite link**: in the app's **Settings**, open the repo and press **Copy invite link**, then send it.
3. **They open the link**, make their own token (the app shows them the steps for that repo), and paste it.

## What they see

The repo is added on their device with all its settings. The link carries no token and no notes.

- **A whole team, no links**: press **Share with everyone** once instead. Everyone who can save to the repo then gets it by themselves ([Add a vault](Add%20a%20vault.md)).
- **Read only**: someone who can't save to the repo sees it with a *Read only* badge, without Edit or Save.
