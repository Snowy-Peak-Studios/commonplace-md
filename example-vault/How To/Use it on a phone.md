# Use it on a phone

> [!TIP]
> **tl;dr:** Installing is optional: from the browser's menu or **Share** button. On iPhone and iPad, set up your vaults once more inside it.

Commonplace.md installs like an app: its own icon and window, opening straight to your notes.

## Install it

- **Android** (Chrome): the menu (⋮) › **Install app** or **Add to home screen**.
- **iPhone and iPad** (Safari): the **Share** button › **Add to Home Screen**.
- **A computer** (Chrome, Edge and the like): the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

## The first time

- On Android and on a computer, the installed app shares the browser's storage: your token and vaults are already there, local ones too.
- On iPhone and iPad it has storage of its own: [connect](Connect%20to%20GitHub.md) and [add your vaults](Add%20a%20vault.md) once more inside it. A vault kept on the device in Safari stays in Safari: start your local vault inside the installed app instead.

## Good to know

It updates by itself when the app does. A vault on GitHub keeps a copy on the phone, so it opens with no connection, and saves wait until you're online. A vault only on this device needs no connection at all; installing isn't a backup, so use **Download a copy** now and then. For GitHub, make a token for this device of its own, so you can take it back alone if the phone is lost.
