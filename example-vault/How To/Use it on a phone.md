# Use it on a phone

Commonplace.md installs like an app: its own icon and window, opening straight to your notes.

## Install it

- **Android** (Chrome): the menu (⋮) › **Install app** or **Add to home screen**.
- **iPhone and iPad** (Safari): the **Share** button › **Add to Home Screen**.
- **A computer** (Chrome, Edge and the like): the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

## The first time

- On Android and on a computer, the installed app shares the browser's storage: your token and vaults are already there.
- On iPhone and iPad it has storage of its own: [connect](Connect%20to%20GitHub.md) and [add your vaults](Add%20a%20vault.md) once more inside it.

## Good to know

It updates by itself when the app does. It needs a connection: your notes stay on GitHub. Make a token for this device of its own, so you can take it back alone if the phone is lost.
