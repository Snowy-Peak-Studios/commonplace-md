# Apple oat jars

Makes two breakfast jars.

- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1 cup milk or an oat drink
- 1 apple
- 2 tablespoons plain yoghurt
- A pinch of cinnamon

1. Grate the apple. Mix everything together and divide it between two jars.
2. Cover and leave in the fridge overnight.
