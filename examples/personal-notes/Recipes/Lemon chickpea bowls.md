# Lemon chickpea bowls

Makes two bowls.

- 1 tin of chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 2 portions of cooked rice
- 1 lemon
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 handfuls baby spinach

1. Warm the chickpeas in the oil in a frying pan.
2. Add the spinach and stir until it wilts.
3. Divide over rice and squeeze the lemon on top.
